# Controls and contested restart update — November 3, 2026

Local changes; publication hold remains active. No deployment or online service activation.

- A gold ring, dashed pass line and shirt number identify the actual proposed receiver. E, gamepad LB or touch TARGET cycles eligible teammates and locks the selection while the same player carries. Invalid, stunned, dead, benched or out-of-range targets fall back to directional selection. J / Space / gamepad A / touch PASS passes to that player.
- Hold K / gamepad B / touch SHOOT to charge, steer up/down to aim, release to shoot. A cyan line marks the nominal aim; existing accuracy/spread rules still apply. Focus loss, pause and loss of possession cancel a charge.
- Off-ball rings show knuckle reach (1.45 metres) and defender chain reach (2.8 metres). J / A uses knuckles; K / B uses a defender chain or falls back to knuckles for other field players. Goalkeepers retain their existing automatic behavior. Hit/miss and loose-ball messages clarify outcomes; off-ball injuries remain fouls.
- Explosion cutaways name the opposing replacement recipient and the 15-second timer. Loose ball pickup remains automatic.
- After a goal celebration, one eligible field player per team has a short knuckle fight for possession at centre court. A 1.5-second preparation interval precedes the whistle. Close in, face the opponent and tap J / K / A / B / KNUCKLES to punch. Hold Shift / RT / BLOCK to guard against a punch from the front. Guarding slows movement and prevents punching until released; attacks already in progress must finish before guarding. Land three unblocked hits to win the 30-second ball and resume team play. Other players wait; the match clock and bomb timer pause. The ball is withheld during the fight, so proximity never awards possession. Both fighters remain on the roster. Simultaneous hits resolve together, with a seeded tie decision for simultaneous knockdowns. Directional guards and fight progress survive online snapshots. The normal off-ball foul rule resumes with team play.
- Touch controls now have positioning, minimum 44-pixel buttons, a receiver button and changing action labels. Small touch screens omit the radar to keep action buttons clear. Portrait and landscape layouts were checked.

Validation: `npm test` runs static/mobile/privacy checks and deterministic gameplay regressions for either challenge winner, AI challenges, excluded participants, held clocks, locked passes, snapshot parity, replacement balls, full time and eight complete AI matches. Browser QA checked desktop, portrait touch and landscape touch, receiver cycling, shot release, restart presentation and button bounds with no page exceptions. Physical controller testing and live multiplayer remain unverified; online play needs the matching simulation and input support on its server.
